Coverage Map

A research tool inside the OHIF CT viewer. While someone reads a scan, it records which organs the screen actually showed, then colors each one on a 3D model as reviewed, partly reviewed or skipped.

In 18 scripted readings it caught all 59 planted skips. OHIF’s built-in tracker caught 28.

Limits. Research use only. Not a medical device.

Python with aiohttp and TotalSegmentator, TypeScript, OHIF and Cornerstone3D, Orthanc, vtk.js, Pyodide
