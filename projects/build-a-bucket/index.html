Build a Bucket

A mobile-first rebuild of the Build a Bucket NBA game. You make ten picks, spin a team and play an 82-game season through the playoffs. It adds daily, salary-cap and squad modes, plus shared online leagues for 2 to 8 friends.

Player and team data load from the original game’s public repository.

Vite, Preact, TypeScript, Neon Postgres, Vercel
