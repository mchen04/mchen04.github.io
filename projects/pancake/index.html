Pancake

A dynasty fantasy basketball app for iPhone, originally built by Jeremy.

Contribution

Ongoing maintenance, optimization and updates to the app.

What the app does

Leagues carry rosters and draft picks from season to season. The app supports startup auctions, rookie drafts, daily lineups, FAAB waiver claims, multi-team trades and live scoring.

It's installable as a web app on iPhone, with web push notifications.

Stack

TypeScript, Expo Router and React Native Web, with Supabase for authentication, realtime updates, PostgreSQL and Edge Functions.
