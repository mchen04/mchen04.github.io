Poker Banker

A phone-first ledger for home cash poker games. The banker records buy-ins, cash-outs, payments and corrections, and players follow along through a link with no login.

It records money but never moves it.

TypeScript, Hono, Postgres on Neon, IndexedDB, Vercel
